Getting started with LaneKeep

LaneKeep remembers, for each set of displays, which app's windows go on which display, where, and at
what size, and puts them back. It lives in the menu bar (top right of the screen) and has no Dock icon.
Its screens are in Japanese.
It does not use the internet. The layouts it remembers are saved only on this Mac
(~/Library/Application Support/LaneKeep/).


1. Drag "LaneKeep" onto the "アプリケーション" (Applications) shortcut.

2. The first time you open LaneKeep, macOS asks you to confirm it.
   LaneKeep isn't registered with Apple's paid developer program, so at first macOS says it can't be opened.

   macOS 15 or later:
     1) Open "LaneKeep" in Applications. When the warning “LaneKeep” Not Opened appears, click "Done".
        Don't click the blue "Move to Trash" button. The warning appears every time you try to open
        LaneKeep, so go on to step 2 instead of opening it again.
     2) Double-click "設定を開く" (Open Settings) in this disk image. It opens System Settings >
        Privacy & Security; scroll down to the "Security" section at the bottom. (Or choose Apple menu >
        System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down.)
     3) Next to “LaneKeep” was blocked to protect your Mac., click "Open Anyway" and confirm with Touch ID
        or your password. (This button is available for about an hour after the warning. If it isn't there,
        start again from step 1.)
     4) When asked Open “LaneKeep”?, click "Open Anyway", the middle button. LaneKeep opens.
        Again, don't click the blue "Move to Trash". (If you're not asked, open LaneKeep again.)

   macOS 14:
     In Finder, Control-click "LaneKeep", choose "Open", then click "Open" again.

   After the first time, it opens normally. After you replace it with a new version, you need to confirm it again.

3. LaneKeep then opens its menu at the top right of the screen and asks for the accessibility permission
   ("ウィンドウを動かす許可が必要です"). Click "システム設定を開く" (Open System Settings) and turn on LaneKeep in
   Privacy & Security > Accessibility. LaneKeep uses it to read the positions of other apps' windows and to
   move them. Once it is on, LaneKeep moves on by itself. If it still says the permission is missing
   ("まだ許可されていません"), click "あとで" (Later) and then "終了" (Quit) in its menu, and open it again.

4. Using it
   - Arrange your windows, then choose "今の配置を保存…" (Save the current layout) in the menu and name it.
   - To put them back, click "復元" (Restore) in the list, or press the workspace's shortcut (⌃⌥1, ⌃⌥2, …).
     ⌃⌥L also opens the menu.
   - With "つないだら自動で復元" (Restore automatically on connect) on, connecting the same displays as when you
     saved puts the windows back by itself.
   - To have LaneKeep from the moment you log in, turn on "ログイン時に起動" (Launch at login) under "一般"
     (General) in its settings (the gear in the menu).
